Comprehensive Veterinary Services

Everything your pet needs under one roof—emergency care, surgery, diagnostics, physiotherapy, and preventive medicine.

🚨

Emergency & Critical Care

When your pet's life is on the line, every second counts. Our emergency team is trained, equipped, and ready 24/7 to stabilize, diagnose, and treat life-threatening conditions with clinical precision and compassion.

What We Handle:

  • •Gastrointestinal Dilatation-Volvulus (GDV) — bloat
  • •Trauma and severe injuries
  • •Toxin ingestion and poisoning
  • •Respiratory distress and choking
  • •Seizures and neurological emergencies

Surgery & Inpatient Care

From routine spaying/neutering to emergency abdominal surgery, our experienced veterinary surgeons work in a sterile operating theatre with full anesthetic monitoring. Patients recover in our ICU with 24x7 nursing care.

Common Procedures:

  • •Spay and neuter
  • •Abdominal surgery (GDV, foreign body, mass removal)
  • •Orthopedic surgery (fractures, ligament repair)
  • •Tumor removal and biopsy
🦵

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Recovery doesn't end with discharge. Our evidence-based physiotherapy programs help pets regain strength, mobility, and quality of life after surgery, injury, or chronic illness.

Conditions We Treat:

  • •Post-operative mobility (after surgery)
  • •Spinal and orthopedic injuries
  • •Arthritis and chronic pain
  • •Neurological conditions

Success Story:

"My German Shepherd couldn't walk after surgery and spinal complications. After physiotherapy with SuperPets, he went from non-ambulatory to running and playing. The regular follow-ups and tailored care made all the difference." — Pet Parent

🔬

Diagnostics & Imaging

Quick, precise diagnosis guides the right treatment. Our in-house lab and imaging equipment mean rapid results and informed decisions—without delays.

Available Services:

  • •Digital X-ray (chest, abdomen, orthopedic)
  • •Ultrasound (abdominal, cardiac)
  • •Blood work (CBC, chemistry, thyroid profiles)
  • •Urinalysis and fecal examination

Why In-House?

  • ✓Results in hours, not days
  • ✓Reduces stress (no external lab transport)
  • ✓Faster treatment decisions
💊

Preventive Care & Wellness

Prevention is the best medicine. Regular wellness exams, vaccinations, and parasite control keep your pet healthy and catch issues early.

Services:

  • •Annual wellness exams
  • •Vaccination programs
  • •Parasite prevention
  • •Dental checks and cleaning
  • •Nutrition counseling
  • •Microchipping
🦷

Dentistry & Dermatology

Dental Services:

  • •Preventive cleaning
  • •Tartar removal
  • •Tooth extraction
  • •Dental radiography

Dermatology Services:

  • •Allergy management
  • •Ear infection treatment
  • •Skin infection treatment
  • •Nutritional consultation
