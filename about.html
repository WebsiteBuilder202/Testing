Our Journey

2022

Founding Vision

SUPERPETS was founded with a vision to create a veterinary hospital that combines medical excellence with compassionate care. Our founders recognized the need for 24/7 emergency services and comprehensive specialty care under one roof.

2023

Facility Development

We established our state-of-the-art facility in Khar West, Mumbai, equipped with modern surgical suites, ICU monitoring, diagnostic imaging, and physiotherapy rehabilitation centers. Every detail was designed for optimal pet care and family comfort.

2024

Team Building

We assembled a team of experienced veterinary specialists, emergency care experts, and compassionate support staff. Each team member shares our commitment to excellence and our inclusive, welcoming approach to pet care.

2025

Growth & Recognition

Today, SUPERPETS serves hundreds of families with a 4.2★ rating from 792+ reviews. We've become known for our exceptional emergency care, successful complex surgeries, and life-changing physiotherapy outcomes. Our second facility is planned to serve even more families.
