Emergency Conditions We Treat

⚠️

GDV (Bloat)

Life-threatening stomach twisting. Immediate surgery required.

Signs: Restlessness, unproductive vomiting, distended belly
🏥

Trauma & Injuries

Accidents, falls, bites. Stabilization and surgical care.

Includes: Fractures, wounds, internal injuries
☠️

Toxin Ingestion

Poisonous substances, human medications, toxic foods.

Common: Chocolate, rat poison, household cleaners
🫁

Respiratory Distress

Breathing difficulties, choking, airway obstructions.

Critical: Oxygen therapy may be needed immediately
⚡

Seizures

Neurological emergencies, epilepsy, toxin reactions.

Multiple seizures or prolonged episodes are emergencies
💔

Cardiac Emergency

Heart failure, arrhythmias, severe dehydration.

ICU monitoring and cardiac support available

Our Emergency Protocol

1

Immediate Triage

Assess severity, stabilize airway/breathing/circulation

2

Rapid Diagnostics

X-ray, ultrasound, bloodwork (on-site)

3

Treatment

Medication, fluids, surgery, or intensive monitoring

4

Recovery

ICU care until stable, follow-up planning

⚠️ SEEK IMMEDIATE CARE IF:

  • 🚨 Difficulty breathing or choking
  • 🚨 Unconscious or non-responsive
  • 🚨 Severe bleeding that won't stop
  • 🚨 Seizures lasting more than 2 minutes
  • 🚨 Suspected poisoning
  • 🚨 Distended, hard abdomen (possible GDV)
