Frequently Asked Questions

🏥 Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes! Our emergency team is on-site 24/7 to handle urgent cases. Routine appointments are also available during extended hours, 7 days a week.

🚗 Is parking available at your facility?

Yes, parking is available at the Mee Mee Tower facility. Ask our team for directions to the nearest parking when you arrive.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and digital payments (UPI, Paytm, Google Pay). Payment plans are available for major procedures.

🏳️‍🌈 Are you LGBTQ+ friendly?

Absolutely! SUPERPETS is proud to be LGBTQ+ friendly and women-owned. Everyone is welcome here, and we strive to create a safe, inclusive environment for all families and their beloved pets.

⏰ How quickly can I get an appointment?

Emergency cases are seen immediately. Routine appointments can usually be scheduled within 24-48 hours. We also offer same-day appointments for urgent but non-emergency situations.
